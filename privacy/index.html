Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Fontiful is a keyboard and an app for writing text in styles, symbols and kaomoji. Everything you type stays on your iPhone or iPad. The app sends two kinds of information, both anonymous: crash reports and counts of which features are used. The keyboard sends nothing at all.

The keyboard

  • The Fontiful keyboard has no internet code. It never sends, stores or logs what you type, with or without Full Access.
  • It remembers only your style choice, the styles in its bar and your recent symbols and emoji, on your device.
  • Full Access is optional. iOS shows the same warning for every keyboard. For Fontiful, Full Access lets the keyboard read the saved texts, theme, photo background and keyboard bar you set in the app, and play key clicks and haptics. That is all it is used for.
  • In password fields iOS always uses Apple's keyboard.

Usage counts

The app (not the keyboard) counts which features are used, such as "a style was copied" or "Captions was opened", with TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH). These counts never contain your text, saved texts, names or anything you typed; numbers are sent as ranges. They are anonymous, not linked to you, not used for advertising and not shared.

Crash reports

If the App Store or TestFlight version of the app crashes or stops responding, it sends a crash report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), so the problem can be found and fixed. A report contains the technical details of the crash: the app version, the code that was running, the device model, iOS version, free memory and similar device state, and a random install identifier.

A report does not contain your name, email, your texts, screenshots or a recording of the screen. IP addresses are not stored. Reports are not linked to you, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else. About one in ten sessions also sends performance timings for the same purpose. The keyboard never sends crash reports.

Photos

If you choose a photo background, you pick one photo with Apple's photo picker; Fontiful sees only that photo. The app saves a small, cropped copy for the keyboard on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

What is stored on your device

  • Your saved texts, keyboard settings, theme and photo background, shared between the app and the keyboard on your device.
  • Delete the app to remove all of it.

What Fontiful does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking across apps, no location, no contacts, no microphone.

Children

Fontiful does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
