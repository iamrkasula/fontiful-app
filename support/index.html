The keyboard

How do I add the keyboard?

Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard… › Fontiful. In the app, Settings › Set up the keyboard again walks you through it.

How do I switch to it?

In any app, touch and hold the globe key under the keyboard and pick Fontiful.

Do I need Full Access?

No. Typing in every style, symbols and kaomoji work without it. Full Access adds your saved texts, themes, photo background and key clicks.

Is what I type sent anywhere?

No. The keyboard has no internet code and keeps nothing you type.

Styles

Why does a style show boxes in one app?

That app uses a font without those characters. Every style in Fontiful works in Apple's apps; try another style in that app.

Why can't I use it in password fields?

iOS always uses Apple's keyboard for passwords.
